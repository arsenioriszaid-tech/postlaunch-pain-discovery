# POST-LAUNCH PAIN DISCOVERY — Executive Finding

**Question asked:** what repeatedly breaks for technical founders after launch, how they cope, what it costs, why existing solutions under-resolve it, and whether an AI agent can remove part of the recurring work — with Ads as one distribution sub-category, never the assumed answer.

**What the evidence actually supports:**
1. The densest pain sits DOWN-funnel, not in traffic volume: signups that never pay (industry medians 8% free→paid [23], 3% trial→paid [24]), pre-value friction fixed by deletion not copy (2.5%→14.6% [22]), churn ignored while acquisition is celebrated [33], cancellation data that lies [32], and 20–40% of churn as failed payments [35].
2. "More traffic/ads" is the most common MISDIAGNOSIS: plateaued founders raised traffic without moving revenue [47][48]; $100–$10k ad burns amplify unproven offers [41][42][43][44][45][46]; $100K/mo ads failed where $0 retention work halved churn [36]; the loudest early complaint (zero users [1][2][3]) is most often an offer/positioning/funnel problem [5].
3. The highest-recurrence tax is founder-operations: 1–3 hrs/day support [26][29], same-5-questions mornings [27], 2h/customer onboarding [60] — bounded, daily, verifiable agent-fit with a sequencing guardrail (white-glove first [61], automate second [62]).
4. Cold-outreach and content-volume automation are KILLED as theses: winners exit outbound for warm/manual [13][15][21][38]; the content-volume game decayed per its inventor [53][54].

**What it does NOT support:** a single winner product; any build decision (WTP, prevalence %, integration willingness all unmeasured — see `07-validation-plan.md`); ads as a top pain; fully-autonomous support/positioning agents.

**Ranked opportunity order (formula-locked, evidence-only, no winner):** O1 funnel-friction audit (30) > O2 churn-truth + dunning (28) > O3 founder-ops shift-plan (26) > O4 positioning analyst (24) > O5 launch sequencing (22) > O6 ads-gate (16, feature-only) > O7 consistency-OS (15, do-not-build).
