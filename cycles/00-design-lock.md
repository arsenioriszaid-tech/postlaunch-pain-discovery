# CYCLE 0 — Research Design Lock (POST-LAUNCH PAIN DISCOVERY RESEARCH OS)

Date: 2026-09-23. Target: technical founders / indie hackers / small software teams, product production-ready + launched.
Scope: discovery → positioning → distribution (ads = sub-category only) → acquisition → conversion → activation → retention → feedback/learning → support → operations.

## Source hierarchy (locked)
1. First-person + behavioral: Indie Hackers, Reddit, HN, X/Twitter build-in-public, GitHub discussions/issues, Product Hunt discussions, launch retrospectives, founder interviews.
2. Secondary behavioral: G2/Capterra/marketplace/community reviews.
3. Tertiary context only: reports/articles. SEO articles NEVER primary pain proof.

## Evidence rule (locked)
Per row: source | date | URL | founder/user type | product type | stage | exact pain | triggering workflow | current workflow | frequency | time/cost | attempted solution | workaround | money spent | outcome | quote/paraphrase | strength (A/B/C) + type tag (stated/behavioral/economic/workaround). No opinion→fact upgrades. No duplicate-source double counting. Correlation ≠ causation.

## Hypotheses under test (no assumed answer)
- H1: "No users/no traction after launch" is the most frequent stated pain.
- H2: Distribution-channel selection (not ads) is the bottleneck; ads is minor.
- H3: Traffic→signup and signup→payment leaks dominate over raw traffic shortage.
- H4: Activation/onboarding drop-off is a recurring, time-costly workflow.
- H5: Churn/retention + conflicting feedback → product-direction paralysis.
- H6: Support/ops burden eats founder time weekly (solvable by automation).
- H7: Content/outreach burden is high-frequency but saturated with tools.
- H8: Paid-ads pain is real but narrow (wasted spend, confusing optimization), NOT the top pain.
- H9 (kill): Most "marketing pains" are upstream product/positioning/onboarding failures misattributed.
- H10 (kill): Seemingly frequent complaints are novice noise; experienced founders dismiss them.

## Scoring model (locked, 1–5 each; penalties subtracted)
Score = (Frequency + Severity + EconomicImpact + Recurrence + WorkaroundIntensity + WTPsignal + AI-AgentSuitability + Accessibility) − (CompetitionSaturation + TechnicalIntegrationComplexity).
Each 1–5 with written justification + raw evidence links. No subjective "market attractiveness" score.
