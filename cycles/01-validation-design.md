# Validation Design Lock (autonomous desk-validation phase, 2026-09-23)

Starting corpus: ledger [1]–[145], hypothesis matrix (H1–H10 + kills), 07-validation-plan, P1–P8, O1–O7. No interviews. No product proposals.
Stopping condition: additional desk research yields diminishing new information AND each leading hypothesis has an evidence-backed case for/against a real-world test.

## Hypotheses under validation (leading = O1/O2/O3-mapped)
- VH1 (P3): Pre-value funnel friction is a recurring, fixable conversion bottleneck for post-launch SaaS (not generic frustration).
- VH2 (P4): Churn-truth gap (lying surveys + ignored involuntary churn) recurs and costs measurable MRR.
- VH3 (P8): Founder support/ops tax recurs daily at micro scale and resists one-time fixes.
- VH4 (merge test): P1 (silence) + P2 (positioning) + P5 (outreach-zero) are symptoms of one upstream problem (offer/funnel), not independent pains.
- VH5 (economics): Founders show economic commitment (paid $) for VH1–VH3-shaped relief — not just complaints.
- VH6 (kill-2): O1–O3 survivors are NOT already solved by incumbents (audit tools, dunning, Intercom Fin-class AI).

## Falsification criteria (pre-registered; a triggered criterion kills/narrows)
- KILL VH1 if: ≥3 independent cases where friction removal/onboarding redo did NOT move conversion, OR conversion variance fully explained by traffic-source quality in ≥3 cases.
- KILL VH2 if: exit-interview programs systematically fail to transfer into retention gains (≥3 failures), OR involuntary share measures small (<10%) at micro scale in ≥2 quantified sources.
- KILL VH3 if: support load proves one-time-fixable (docs → sustained ~zero) in ≥3 cases with follow-up, OR AI triage commoditized (founders report Fin-class tools fully absorb it, ≥3).
- MERGE VH4 if: P1/P2/P5 cases co-occur with funnel/offer defects in >2/3 of new sampled cases → collapse into single upstream hypothesis.
- KILL VH5 if: no "$X paid" social-proof found after dedicated WTP search (absence logged as finding, not failure to search).
- KILL any survivor (VH6) if: an incumbent demonstrably owns the exact loop (founder evidence, not vendor claims) in ≥3 independent reports.

## Rules
Stated vs behavioral vs economic tags mandatory. No syndication double-counting (same story republished = 1). Source-count is NOT the goal; diminishing-returns stop applies. Counterevidence required per stream. No invented URLs/quotes/stats; grade A/B/C.
