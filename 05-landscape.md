# Competitor & Workaround Landscape (CYCLE 4)

Stream-H mapped 20 tools (transcript `evidence/stream-7.txt` + spot-checks). Grades are vendor-context (C), not pain proof. Saturation = how routinely founders mention/already-tried it in Sec. A–G evidence.

## Saturated (sell the measurement, founders still stuck on the fix)
| Category | Names | Workflow claimed removed | Pricing signal | Saturation signal | Gap founders still report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product analytics | PostHog, Mixpanel | Juggling GA+replay+flags+surveys | Free tier → $$/event | Mentioned as default stack everywhere | Shows drop-off, not causal friction (P3 gap) |
| Helpdesk chat | Intercom, Crisp, Help Scout | Shared inbox + chat | Per-seat $$ | Default recommendation in support threads | Seats ≠ shift-plan; no billing/onboarding/health choreography (P8 gap) |
| Lifecycle email | Customer.io, Loops, Mailchimp | Sequences, broadcasts | Free→$$/contact | Assumed present | Sequences exist; runners can't diagnose leak [22] |
| Builders/pages | Webflow, Framer | Landing pages | Free→$/site | Ubiquitous | Structure without message (P2 gap) |
| SEO suites | Ahrefs, Semrush | Keyword/content research | $$$ (~$99+/mo) | Standard advice | Data without founder-fit execution OS (P7) |
| Social schedulers | Buffer, Hypefury/Typefully | Cross-posting, consistency | $/mo | Standard advice | Generation saturated; trust-channel fit missing (G10) |
| Launch directories | Product Hunt, BetaList | Launch visibility | Free | Tried-and-disputed [49][50][51][52] | Entertainment-grade conversion |
| Labor | Upwork, agencies, studios | Done-for-you GTM/positioning | $$$$ | Cited for sprints [19] | Cost + quality variance; 2 support hires burned [29] |

## Thin / least-covered (3 gaps consolidated with stream H)
1. **Cancel-moment truth capture** — exit-live-conversation + stated-vs-usage reconciliation at micro scale (P4). Incumbents measure (Baremetrics/ChartMogul) or survey (NPS tools — the lying layer [32]); none run the reconcile loop for a solo founder.
2. **Micro-SaaS failed-payment recovery** — dunning-grade stacks are enterprise-priced/operated; 20–40% churn share [35] with no founder-scale packaged loop.
3. **Pre-value friction audit** — analytics-adjacent but judgment-heavy: which ask/step kills WHOM, with removal proposals + test plan (P3). CRO agencies do it at $$$$; no self-serve founder loop.

## Workaround economy (what founders actually do instead of buying)
Manual Reddit/DM grind [13][15], white-glove heroics [61], spreadsheet dunning, 4-template support [27], SEO-after-ads [41][43], founder-led exit chats [31], survey-then-regret [32]. Every surviving opportunity must displace one of THESE with less founder time — not out-feature a saturated incumbent.
