# Research Gaps + Human Validation Plan (before ANY build)

## Gaps this desk research cannot close
1. No prevalence denominators (what % of launched founders hit each pain; forum-selection bias toward complainers).
2. No hr/day or $/mo distributions across MRR bands (support tax, outreach time, ad waste at scale).
3. No WTP proof (nobody observed paying for O1–O3-shaped solutions; agency prices are adjacent, not direct).
4. Conflicting-feedback adjudication workflow is thin (H5-paralysis mechanics barely observed directly).
5. Experience stratification missing (novice vs veteran pain splits inferred, not measured).
6. Integration willingness unknown (will founders grant funnel/billing/email access to an agent?).

## 10 interview questions (5–10 required; ask founders $0–$30k MRR, post-launch ≥8 wks)
1. Walk me through last week's post-launch hours — where did each hour actually go? (closes gap 2; tests P8 recurrence)
2. Show me your signup→paid numbers. What did you last change to move them, and what happened? (tests P3; O1 demand)
3. Tell me about your last 5 cancellations — what reason did they give vs what do YOU believe? (tests P4 truth-gap [32])
4. What share of lost revenue is failed payments vs active cancels — and what do you do about each? (tests involuntary-blindness [35])
5. When signups stalled, how did you decide between "more traffic" vs "fix the page/offer"? (tests H9 misattribution)
6. What did your last $500–$2,000 of growth spend buy, in customers? Would you spend it again? (tests P6; O6 scope)
7. What does support cost you weekly — hours, context-switches, delayed roadmap? Show me the inbox. (tests P8; O3 WTP)
8. Which positioning/ICP decision was hardest, who made it, and what evidence did you use? (tests P2; O4 shape)
9. Which tool did you buy for a post-launch pain and abandon — why? (tests saturation; displacement bar per 05-landscape)
10. If an agent took ONE recurring weekly workflow off your plate but needed read-access to product/billing/email, which workflow and what would you pay? (closes gaps 3+6; direct O1–O3 validation)

## Validation protocol
- n ≥ 12 founders, stratified: 4 pre-revenue, 4 $1–10k MRR, 4 $10k+ MRR. Record workflow walkthroughs, not opinions.
- Kill rule: if <4/12 name the same workflow unprompted AND <3/12 offer concrete WTP, do not build — return to discovery.
- Log every interview against evidence IDs (A1–H, P1–P8) so desk ↔ field stays auditable.
