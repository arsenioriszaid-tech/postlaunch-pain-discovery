# POST-LAUNCH PAIN MAP (all domains, evidence-linked)

Ledger: `/root/.hermes/cache/citations/ledger.json` ([1]–[62]). Detail rows: `01-evidence-table.md`.

## 1. Discovery — "nobody knows it exists"
Day-zero obscurity dominates first-founder reports: 0 users at launch [1][2][3][4], free offer still 0 signups [7], 74 days $0 despite all channels live [5]. Workaround that repeats: manual community participation (Reddit value-first → 10 paying [15]; validation-post → first 100 [13]) and founder-led outreach (Loom 3k adopters [11]). Launch-platform spikes (PH/HN) convert at entertainment rates and are widely disputed [16][49][50][51][52].

## 2. Positioning — "can't tell which problem it is"
Founders cannot self-diagnose distribution vs positioning vs product [1]; commenters point at positioning/urgency gaps in crowded categories; offer-first reframing ("amplify existing desire" [5]) recurs. Systematic ICP work is rare: gut-ICP → months messaging the wrong people [21]; firmographic ICP missed buyer-level problems even at $50M scale [17]; felt-emotion ("relief") rewrites beat polished docs [18]. Positioning advice itself is called vacuous ("a vibe, or a lucky headline" [19]).

## 3. Distribution — channel selection, not ads
Working channels in evidence: warm referrals/inbound [38], Reddit participation [15][13], direct email/DM [11][13], SEO-after-paid-failure [41][43]. Failing/uneconomic: cold outbound at volume (10.8k→0 [38]; 1k→1 meeting [40]), paid ads as substitute for demand [41][42][43][44][45][46], PH launches for real customers [49][50][51]. One-man-SaaS evidence: SEO decays, CPC uneconomic without subscriptions, Reddit replies risk bans — all three failed simultaneously [12].

## 4. Acquisition — traffic that doesn't convert
Traffic-without-signups is attributed to message/proof/path/distribution-fit leaks, not volume [59]; "more traffic amplifies reality" [48]; 1,800 sessions → 12 customers case [55]. Counter-pattern: founders chase volume while the funnel kills every opportunity [48][47].

## 5. Conversion — signup without payment
Free-plan interest with ~0 paid conversion [6]; industry medians 8% free→paid [23], 3% trial→paid [24] — i.e. 9-in-10 non-conversion is NORMAL, but founders experience it as failure. Fix pattern is friction removal, not copy/pricing: 2.5%→14.6% by deleting pre-value steps [22]; doubled trial→paid in 3 wks via onboarding [56].

## 6. Activation — time-to-first-value gap
Pre-value info-asks and optional steps strangle activation [22]; manual white-glove onboarding (400x manual API triggers [61]; 2h/customer [60]) buys learning + conversion early but breaks past ~8 customers. Tracsio: manual onboarding is the fastest learning channel, automation later [62].

## 7. Retention — the back-door leak
Acquisition obsession while equal numbers churn ("down escalator" [33]); cancel-moment conversations beat content/outreach/PH combined [31]; cancellation surveys actively lie (usage data contradicts stated reasons [32]); involuntary/failed-payment churn is 20–40% of total [35] — a purely operational leak most small teams ignore (D4).

## 8. Feedback / learning — which signal to trust
Survey/NPS data misdirects roadmap [32]; conflicting "fix positioning" advice without method [19]; silent cancellations give no reason [34]. Working loop in evidence: usage-data + cancel-moment conversation [31][32]; BuildPad Reddit feedback exchange [13].

## 9. Customer support — founder-time tax
>2 hrs/day support cannibalizes product/sales/sanity [26]; same-5-questions mornings [27]; 3 hrs/day + 2 burned support hires [29]; 2-person-team strain [28]; refunds/chargebacks ops [30]. Workaround stack: docs-deflect → 2x/day async inbox → Loom escalate; ~4 templates ≈ 80% tickets [27].

## 10. Operations — founder-is-the-system
Per-customer onboarding 2h → 15-min systematized [60]; founder as support+billing+health-detection until ops rebuild [60]; manual API-call heroics to fake "instant" [61]. Breaks at ~8 concurrent customers (Founder's-Best-Friend corroboration in search).

## 11. Ads (distribution sub-category — NOT the main pain)
Evidence is consistent: paid ads fail as demand substitute for early SaaS ($2,400→12 signups [41]; $3,200→18 [43]; $3k postmortem [44]; ~$10k lessons [45]; $100→0 [42]; 43-vs-10 metric miscount [46]), succeed only after positioning/funnel fixed (Flow burned $100K/mo pre-fix [36]). Verdict: ads pain is REAL (wasted spend, confusing optimization, misleading platform metrics) but NARROW and DOWNSTREAM — a misattributed symptom in most early cases, not the top pain.
